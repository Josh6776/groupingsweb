About Groupings

Groupings is a daily word grouping puzzle for iPhone, built around one honest puzzle a day. Players sort 16 words into four hidden groups of four, and watch out for trap tiles that could fit more than one group. Beyond the daily puzzle there are Easy to Expert practice grids, Blitz (race the clock), Endless (how long can you last) and global leaderboards. Groupings is fully available in English and Japanese, with puzzles written for each language.

It is made by one developer in the United Kingdom.
