Play Groupings free in your browser

Sort sixteen words into four hidden groups. No download, no sign-up.

This puzzle needs JavaScript. Or get the app below.

How to play

  1. Pick four words that share something in common.
  2. Press Submit. A correct group lights up in its colour.
  3. You have three mistakes. "One away..." means three of your four are right.
  4. The colours run from yellow (easiest) through green and blue to purple (trickiest).
  5. Blitz, Endless and Sudden Death are runs: puzzle after puzzle, and your score is the number of groups you clear.
