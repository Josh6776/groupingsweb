How to play

Sort sixteen words into four hidden groups.

The basics

  • Pick four words that share something in common.
  • Press Submit. A correct group lights up in its colour.
  • Three mistakes and the puzzle is over. "One away..." means three of your four are right.
  • The colours run from yellow (easiest) through green and blue to purple (trickiest).

The modes

  • Daily. One new puzzle a day, the same for everyone.
  • Practice. Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert puzzles to work through.
  • Blitz. You have 45 seconds. Each group adds 5 seconds and each wrong guess costs 4.
  • Endless. Puzzle after puzzle, with 3 mistakes for the whole run.
  • Sudden Death. One mistake and your run is over.

Your score

  • In Blitz, Endless and Sudden Death your score is the number of groups you clear. Your best is saved in this browser.
