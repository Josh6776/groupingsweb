The Daily Puzzle

A fresh grid, every day

Each day brings a brand-new daily puzzle. Sort sixteen words into four hidden groups of four. Puzzles rotate every day and no two are ever the same.

Easy · Medium · Hard · Expert

Play at your level

New to grouping puzzles? Warm up on Easy, then climb through Medium, Hard and Expert as you sharpen up. And keep an eye out for the traps. They're probably not what you think. 😉

Blitz

Think you're fast?

Race the clock in Blitz. Get a group right and earn more time; get it wrong and watch the seconds slip away. How many can you clear before time runs out?

Endless

It goes on, and on, and on

One grid after another with no finish line. Keep your lives alive and see just how far you can go.

Sudden Death

One slip and it's over

No second chances. Make a single mistake and your run is done. Trap-heavy puzzles, back to back, and the biggest coin rewards in the game. How many groups can you clear?

The Gauntlet

Easy start, brutal finish

Climb through the stages from Easy all the way up to Expert, with only three mistakes to last the whole run. Every stage pays more than the last.

The Leaderboard

Claim the top spot

See where you rank. Blitz, Endless, Sudden Death and Gauntlet players compete to be crowned champion on four separate boards. Can you reach the top spot, and more importantly, can you stay there?
