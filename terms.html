14. Rush duels

Rush is a head-to-head mode. A player plays a grid and posts the result, another player plays the same grid, and the better result (taking your time and mistakes into account) wins.

Virtual buy-ins and prizes. Each duel has a buy-in, paid in coins when you start. The winner receives the pot, a draw returns both buy-ins, and the loser's buy-in is lost. Coins have no real-world value and cannot be bought with money (see section 6).

Rating. Rush has a rating that goes up and down with your results. Ratings are for fun and are not guaranteed to stay the same; we may adjust or reset them, for example when we change how Rush works.

Leaving counts as a loss. If you leave a duel, close the app, lose your connection or your device turns off before you finish, the duel counts as a loss and your buy-in is not returned. If nobody plays a grid you posted within 24 hours, your buy-in is returned. If an opponent takes a seat but does not finish within 12 minutes, you win.

Fair play. You must not use more than one account, arrange results with another player, use tools or modified apps, or otherwise manipulate duels, ratings or invites. We may cancel duels, change or reset ratings and coins, remove entries from the Rush leaderboard, or suspend or delete accounts that break these rules (section 3 also applies).

Invites. You can invite other Rush players to a duel, and you can switch invites off in Rush at any time. Invites are limited, so a player can only be invited once every few minutes. Do not use invites to harass anyone; you can report a player as described in section 4.

Availability. Rush may need a recent version of the app, and we may change, pause or end Rush at any time.
